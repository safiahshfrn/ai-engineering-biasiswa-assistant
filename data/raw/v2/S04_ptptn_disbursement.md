## After the offer

An offer is valid for 14 days from approval. The signed, stamped agreement must be submitted within 14 days at the student's institution or the nearest PTPTN branch. The first payment is made after the complete agreement documents are received, and money is credited within 21 working days.

## Continuing payments and withholding

Later payments require the student to remain active on the approved level, programme and institution and to have a GPA of at least 2.0 in the previous semester. Payment is withheld if the GPA is below 2.0, if study is deferred, or if the institution suspends the student. The loan is paid only into the student's own account, not to a parent.
