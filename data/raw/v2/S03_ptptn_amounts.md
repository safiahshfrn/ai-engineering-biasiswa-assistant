## Loan tier by household income

The maximum loan applies when the student or guardian is listed as a Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR) recipient. Families not in the STR data with income up to RM8,000 receive 75% of the maximum, and those above RM8,000 receive 50% of the maximum.

## Annual full-time amounts (RM per year: maximum / 75% / 50%)

IPTA, polytechnic and community college, diploma or integrated programme: 4,750 / 3,560 / 2,380.
IPTA, bachelor's degree (science): 6,650 / 4,990 / 3,330.
IPTA, bachelor's degree (arts): 6,180 / 4,630 / 3,090.
IPTS, foundation: 6,800 / 5,100 / 3,400.
IPTS, diploma: 6,800 / 5,100 / 3,400.
IPTS, diploma in pharmacy, dentistry or health sciences: 12,750 / 9,560 / 6,380.
IPTS, bachelor's degree (science): 14,030 / 10,520 / 7,010.
IPTS, bachelor's degree (arts): 13,600 / 10,200 / 6,800.
IPTS, bachelor's degree (health sciences): 17,000 / 12,750 / 8,500.
IPTS, bachelor's degree in medicine (MBBS), dentistry or veterinary science: 50,000 / 37,500 / 30,000.
