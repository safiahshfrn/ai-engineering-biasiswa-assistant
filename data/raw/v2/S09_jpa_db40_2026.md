## Who can apply

Dermasiswa B40 (DB40) 2026 is a JPA bursary for SPM 2025 candidates from low-income (B40) families who continue to diploma study in Malaysia. The applicant must be a Malaysian citizen who sat SPM 2025 for the first time, and must be under 19 (under 20 for students from the transition class) on 31 December of the exam year.

Household per-capita income must be in the B40 category, which the statement gives as RM5,249 a month or less. The applicant must be free of chronic or infectious illness that could affect study, and neither the applicant nor the parents may be blacklisted by JPA or under JPA legal action.

## Where and what

Places are at selected public universities, polytechnics, MARA TVET institutions and public skills training institutes (ILKA), in engineering and technology, TVET, social sciences and arts, science, and architecture. The statement does not say whether an admission offer is needed before applying.

## Dates and channel

Applications were open from 3 August 2026 (2 pm) to 16 August 2026 (5 pm) through the JPA sponsorship portal. Results could be checked from 13 to 22 October 2026, and 8,054 places were offered for 2026.
