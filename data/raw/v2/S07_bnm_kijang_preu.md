## Who can apply

The Kijang Pre-University Scholarship is for Malaysian citizens who have just completed SPM and are no older than 19 on 1 July 2026. The academic requirement is at least 8 As (A+ or A only) in the latest SPM, together with a CEFR result; the page does not define the CEFR level. Leadership, teamwork, co-curricular record and interpersonal skills are also assessed.

The page does not state that an admission offer is needed before applying. Scholars study at pre-university colleges approved by BNM.

## Benefits and obligations

The scholarship pays tuition fees, a subsistence allowance and related allowances. Moving on to undergraduate study depends on meeting the Kijang Undergraduate Programme requirements. After graduating, scholars serve BNM for two years for every year of study. Only shortlisted candidates are contacted.
