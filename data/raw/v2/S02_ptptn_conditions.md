## Who can apply

The applicant must be a Malaysian citizen aged 45 or below on the date of application, and must not have a PTPTN loan account that is being handled by a debt-collection agency (APH).

The gross monthly income of the parents or guardian must not exceed RM50,000 after deducting RM250 for each dependant. The income limit has applied since 1 January 2024.

## Admission offer and course conditions

The applicant must already hold an offer from an IPTA, IPTS or polytechnic, having met the entry requirements set by the Ministry of Higher Education (KPT) and MQA. The course must be approved by KPT and registered with PTPTN; only PTPTN-approved courses appear in the online application portal.

At least one semester of study must remain at the time of application. The applicant must have opened a Simpan SSPN Prime or Simpan SSPN Plus account, and only a 15-digit SSPN Prime account can be used for the application.

## Application channel

Applications, results and offer letters are handled through myPTPTN. The loan starts from the semester in which the complete application is received, not from the first semester.
