## Purpose

TESP is a MARA education loan for SPM, certificate (Sijil) and diploma leavers who continue to a diploma or a first degree in fields and local institutions approved by MARA.

## Who can apply

The applicant and at least one parent must be Malaysian citizens with Bumiputera status. The student and parents or guardian must not be on MARA's blacklist. The applicant must not hold another sponsorship or financial aid for the same level of study. The course must be recognised by the Government and the relevant professional bodies. The applicant must not have had a previous sponsorship terminated for disciplinary reasons.

The age limit is below 30 years for diploma study and below 40 years for bachelor's degree study. Priority is given to applicants from B40 and M40 households. The page gives no specific academic grade and no income figure; it says academic conditions follow the policy in force.

## What the page does not state

The page does not state the application period, the application channel, or whether an admission offer is needed before applying.
