## Programmes and places

For 2026, JPA offered 640 sponsorship places to outstanding SPM leavers across four programmes: Program Penajaan Nasional (30 places), Program Khas Jepun, Korea, Perancis dan Jerman (140), Program Khas Lepasan SPM Dalam Negara (200) and Program Khas JPA-MARA (270). Fields include engineering, science and technology, and social sciences.

## Form of sponsorship

All successful candidates are funded under the Academic Merit-Based Convertible Loan (Pinjaman Boleh Ubah Berasaskan Merit Akademik, PBUA), which JPA introduced in June 2025. Results could be checked online from 19 to 22 June 2026.

## What the report does not state

The report does not give the eligibility conditions, SPM grade thresholds or the application period for each programme.
