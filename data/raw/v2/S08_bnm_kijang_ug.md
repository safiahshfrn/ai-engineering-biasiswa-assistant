## Who can apply

The Kijang Undergraduate Scholarship is for Malaysian citizens no older than 22 on 1 July 2026 who have an offer to study at, or are already enrolled in, a university on BNM's approved list.

For school leavers, one of these results is needed: STPM, diploma, matriculation or foundation CGPA of at least 3.50; A-Level with at least 3 As; or another listed international qualification. Applicants also need at least 5 credits in SPM or an equivalent, including Bahasa Melayu, English and Mathematics.

## Fields, window and obligations

Fields include economics, accounting, finance, actuarial science, mathematics, statistics, law, data science and computer science. Applications for the 2026 cycle were open from 17 to 26 April 2026; late applications are not accepted. The scholarship pays tuition fees, subsistence and related allowances, and scholars serve BNM for two years for every year of study.
