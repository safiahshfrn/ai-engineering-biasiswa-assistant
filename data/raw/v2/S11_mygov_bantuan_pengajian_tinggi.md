## Aid listed for SPM leavers

The government portal lists financial aid for SPM leavers going on to higher education. It names Dermasiswa B40 from JPA for SPM leavers from low-income families at diploma and TVET-diploma level, and links to the JPA sponsorship portal for the full conditions.

It also lists the Community College Student Financial Assistance (BKPKK) for low-income community college students, which requires Malaysian citizenship and enrolment in a certificate programme of at least four semesters, and Foundation Financial Assistance (BKA) for B40 and M40 students in foundation programmes at public universities and five selected polytechnics.
