## Coverage by study level and institution

PTPTN education loans pay tuition fees and living costs for students at local higher education institutions only, meaning public institutions (IPTA) and private institutions (IPTS).

Full-time diploma study is financed at IPTA, polytechnics and IPTS. Full-time first-degree (Ijazah Pertama) study is financed at IPTA and IPTS. Diploma Lanjutan is financed at IPTA and polytechnics.

Part-time diploma and first-degree study at IPTS is financed only for students of five named private institutions: Universiti Terbuka Malaysia, Wawasan Open University, Asia e University, UNITAR and UNIRAZAK. Distance-learning (PJJ) diploma study is financed at IPTA only.

Foundation study at a private institution qualifies only at MMU, UNITEN, UTP, UNITAR and UNIRAZAK. A 2+1 twinning programme qualifies, but the loan covers only the years studied in Malaysia.
