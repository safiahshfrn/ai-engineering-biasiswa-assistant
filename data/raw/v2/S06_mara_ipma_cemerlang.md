## Purpose and who can apply

This programme is only for outstanding graduates of MARA education institutions (IPMa) who continue to a diploma or first degree in Malaysia or overseas. Selection is based on academic excellence and active co-curricular involvement during certificate and diploma study at IPMa.

The applicant and at least one parent must be Malaysian citizens with Bumiputera status, must not be on MARA's blacklist, and must not hold another sponsorship for the same level. The academic requirement is a CGPA of 3.90 or higher. Priority is given to the B40 group.
